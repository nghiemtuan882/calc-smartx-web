15 features, seen in action

Every clip is recorded from the app itself, in the language you are reading.

01

Linked calculations

  • Edit a number, results follow

    Tap any number to change it. Every related result recalculates.

  • Keep going from a result

    Tap a result, then + − × ÷. The new line stays linked to that result.

  • Drag to link

    Hold and drag a number: a small card rides above your finger. Drop it on a number to replace it, or below the last line for a new line.

  • Tap a number to link it

    After pressing an operator, tap any number above to bring it in, already linked.

  • Tap a sign to change it

    Tap + − × ÷ in a calculation and pick another sign. The result updates right away.

02

Fast entry and edits

  • Smart ⌫

    Tap ⌫ to delete one character at a time, signs included. With an answer selected, ⌫ jumps into the last number. Hold ⌫ to delete the whole line.

  • 000 key for quick amounts

    Type 12 and tap 000 twice to get 12,000,000 — no need to press 0 six times.

  • Drag an operator to move

    Hold and drag + − × ÷ or = to move the whole calculation.

03

Notes and layout

  • Notes on every number

    Tap the note key and type labels like “unit price” right above the number.

  • Slide the operator column to zoom

    Put a finger on the + − × ÷ column and slide up to zoom in, down to zoom out.

  • Swipe the keypad to adjust spacing

    Swipe sideways along the top row to space out numbers. Swipe up or down the left column to space out lines.

  • Make it yours

    Change the color, font, text size, language, dark mode and left-handed keypad.

04

Capture and share

  • Clean screenshots

    Capture the full screen, an area you draw, or just the lines you pick.

  • Capture just the lines you need

    Pick lines even if they're far apart; the image puts them right next to each other.

  • Send pictures straight to chat apps

    After a capture, save the picture or send it right away via Zalo, WhatsApp, Viber, Telegram or Messenger.
